\subsection{Model Training}
\label{subsec:training}

We train both models end-to-end using a 80/20 train/test split and minimize the MSE-loss $\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{\vert \mathcal{B} \vert}\sum_{(s_t, c_t, v_t) \in \mathcal{B}} (f_{\theta}(s_t, c_t) - v_t)^2$, where $\mathcal{B}$ is a batch with batchsize $\vert \mathcal{B}\vert = 64$ and $f_{\theta}$ is the respective neural network. 
We use Adam as an optimizer.
Since the intents are not equally distributed in the dataset ('lane following' $\approx 73 \%$, 'left' $\approx 11 \%$, 'straight' $\approx 10 \%$, 'right' $\approx 6 \%$), we oversample the 'left', 'right', and 'straight' intents. 
We define a sampling strategy as an array of percentages with the order $[\text{lane following}, \text{ straight}, \text{ left}, \text{ right}]$. 
For our first baseline approach, we use the sampling strategy $[40, 20, 20, 20]$.  
We use the model with the lowest validation loss.

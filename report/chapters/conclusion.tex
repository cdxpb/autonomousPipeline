\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chapter:conclusion}

In conclusion, we demonstrate that our Conditional Imitation Learning models are able to navigate robustly through Duckietown while following commands provided by our VLM-based planner.
Through our experiments we find that both MH and FiLM based CIL models are able to produce good driving policies and that the data sampling strategy matters. We believe our pipeline is a strong foundation which can be extended to handle more complex scenarios and tasks in Duckietown.

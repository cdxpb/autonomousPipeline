\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chapter:abstract}

While implementing Duckietown Learning Experiences (LXs) we experienced the limitations of classical modular pipelines first hand. 
We overcome these limitations by training an end-to-end conditional imitation learning model on segmented images that drives the Duckiebot according to a per-frame intent, produced by a fine-tuned VLM-guided planner following a high-level plan. 
With this setup the bot robustly performs lane-following and turning at intersections, enabling a safe driving experience in Duckietown.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chapter:abstract}

During the work on the Duckietown LX's we experienced problems of classical modular pipelines first hand.
We overcome these limitations by training an end-to-end imitation learning model based on a segmented image input that drives the Duckiebot according to a high level plan that is supplied by a fine-tuned VLM.
With this setup the bot robustly performs lane-following and turning at intersections enabling a safe driving experience in Duckietown.
